title=Project Golf x A-Life
description=[h1]Project Golf x A-Life - Never Golf Alone[/h1]
description=
description=Invite a friendly A-Life survivor for a round at West Maple. They walk the course with you, line up their own shots, swing their own club and keep their own score. Win or lose, it beats talking to the zombies.
description=
description=[b]Requires:[/b] [url=LINK HERE]Project Golf[/url] and [url=LINK HERE]Project A-Life[/url]
description=
description=>>> IMAGE: you and your NPC partner on the tee box at West Maple <<<
description=
description=[h2]How to start a round[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Carry a [b]golf club[/b] and some [b]golf balls[/b]. No club, no golf.
description=[*]Talk to a friendly A-Life NPC and open the [b]Golf[/b] tab: [i]"Fancy a round of golf?"[/i]
description=[*]If they're up for it (a wary stranger might turn you down), they're your partner. They follow you to the tee.
description=[*]Tee off as usual. When your ball stops, it's their turn.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]A real playing partner[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Their own ball, their own swing[/b] - they walk to their side of the tee box, line up and drive with a full two-handed swing. Their ball flies with Project Golf's real physics, so it bounces off trees, runs out on the fairway and sinks into water just like yours.
description=[*][b]They read the hole[/b] - partners look at the fairway, the rough and the trees before every shot. On a dogleg they play around the corner instead of blasting into the woods.
description=[*][b]They take turns[/b] - after each of your shots they walk to their ball and play it. Once you're in, they finish the hole.
description=[*][b]They keep up[/b] - they follow you between shots and jog to catch up if you get ahead. No sprinting on a golf course!
description=[*][b]Their name on their ball[/b] - every ball is labelled, e.g. [i]Dave's golf ball[/i], so you never play the wrong one.
description=[/list]
description=
description=>>> IMAGE: NPC mid-swing, ball in the air <<<
description=
description=[h2]Every golfer is different[/h2]
description=Each NPC has their own [b]golf rating from 1 to 10[/b], shown when the round starts. The same person always has the same rating.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]1[/b] - has never held a club. Wild shots, short drives, plenty of shanks.
description=[*][b]5[/b] - your average weekend golfer.
description=[*][b]10[/b] - the club champion. Long, straight and almost never overswings.
description=[/list]
description=The rating sets their Aiming, their Strength and how often they swing too hard, using the same rules as your own shots. Most people are in the middle. Finding a 10 is rare, so if you do, try to beat them!
description=
description=[h2]Don't want to wait?[/h2]
description=Tired of taking turns? Tell them [i]"Go ahead, take your shot."[/i] and they play the rest of the hole on their own while you watch, or while you go and play yours.
description=
description=[h2]Keep score together[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Your [b]scorecard[/b] gets a column for your partner, with their name and rating: [i]Dave (7)[/i].
description=[*]At the end of every hole you both get the score: [i]Hole 4: you 3 (-1) - Dave 5 (+1)[/i].
description=[*]A partner who's having a bad day picks up at par + 4, so a hole never drags on forever.
description=[/list]
description=
description=>>> IMAGE: the scorecard with the partner column <<<
description=
description=[h2]Calling it a day[/h2]
description=Talk to them and choose [i]"Let's call it a day on the golf."[/i] If you walk far away and leave them behind for a while, the round ends by itself too.
description=
description=[h2]Settings[/h2]
description=[b]Sandbox (page "Project Golf x A-Life"):[/b] [b]Test partner spawn[/b] - off by default. Turn it on to get a right-click option that spawns a friendly golfer next to you, already dressed for the course with a club and balls. Good for trying the mod out or for recording videos. Single player, or admins in multiplayer.
description=

description=[list]
description=[*][b]Build 42[/b] only.
description=[*]Needs [b]Project Golf[/b] and [b]Project A-Life[/b]. Nothing else.
